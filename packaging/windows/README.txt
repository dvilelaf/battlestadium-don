Battle Stadium D.O.N for PC (Windows)
=====================================

This download contains the program only. It contains nothing of the game, and it downloads nothing.
You need your own disc image of the PS2 game (SLPS-25675), made from a disc you own.

1. Unzip this archive anywhere you can write to (for example C:\Games\DON).
2. Run "Battle Stadium DON.exe" from the unzipped folder.
3. Point it at your own disc image when it asks (or run  "Battle Stadium DON.exe" --prepare C:\path\to\your-image.iso ).
   The first preparation translates and compiles the game's code on your PC from your disc; it takes a while
   and the result stays on your PC. Nothing is uploaded.

Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" the first time: the program is not signed with a paid certificate.
Click "More info", then "Run anyway". A graphics card with a Vulkan driver is needed.
